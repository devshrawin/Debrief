ROLE
You are a senior brand and icon designer. Design the refreshed logo system for Debrief (the name stays; do not rename it), a Chrome extension that records a meeting tab, transcribes it (Hindi, English, Malayalam and mixes), and writes the minutes. It runs entirely in the browser: no server, privacy first. The product is being redesigned with a glass material look that follows Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (see REDESIGN-PROMPT.md); the logo must belong to that system.

CURRENT LOGO (the starting point: evolve it, keep what is recognisable, fix what is weak)
Image: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/devshrawin/Debrief/pro/extension/icons/icon512.png (also .../icon.svg in the same folder).
A rounded square in indigo (#3b2fa8) holding a white speech bubble with three vertical waveform bars and a small orange bar on top. Files: extension/icons/icon*.png, extension/icons/icon.svg; generated by scripts/make-icons.mjs.

BRIEF
- Idea to explore: speech turning into a clear written record. Pick ONE strong, simple concept (for example a speech bubble / waveform that resolves into lines of text, a sound wave that becomes a page, a pen-and-voice mark). Avoid microphones, generic robots, sparkle clichés and stock AI imagery.
- Personality: calm, precise, trustworthy, modern. Premium rather than playful.
- Style: a glass app-icon material: translucent layered shapes, a soft inner highlight, a restrained specular edge, a gentle depth shadow. Keep the mark itself flat and bold enough to survive without the glass (the glass is the finish, not the idea).
- Colour: one brand accent (indigo family by default, you may propose a better one) plus one small warm signal colour used only for "recording" (orange or red). Provide light, dark and monochrome versions. Contrast of the mark against its tile at least 4.5:1.
- Apple practice: 1024 px master on a continuous-corner squircle grid, a single clear focal shape, no text inside the icon, generous safe margin, optical alignment, reads at a glance. The mark must also work without the tile.

WHAT TO DELIVER
1. Three concept directions (a short rationale each), then ONE chosen direction with the reasons.
2. The app icon: SVG master (1024), plus PNG exports at 512, 128, 48, 32 and 16 px. Hand-tune the 16 and 32 px versions (fewer details, thicker strokes) so they stay legible in the browser toolbar, in light and dark Chrome themes.
3. Toolbar state variants, same silhouette: idle, "a call is on" (amber glow, files icon16-glow.png, icon32-glow.png, icon48-glow.png), recording (a red recording dot or ring, as a badge-ready version), paused.
4. A wordmark and a horizontal lockup (icon + name), a stacked lockup, and an icon-only monogram. Provide SVG with live-text-free outlines, in full colour, one-colour dark, one-colour light. Pick or build a typeface that fits the Apple type feel (SF-like, geometric humanist); state the font and its licence.
5. Optional motion: a short loop of the mark for the first-run tour and loading states (subtle: waveform bars breathing, or the mark writing itself in). Provide it as SVG/CSS or Lottie-free vanilla animation, with a `prefers-reduced-motion` still.
6. Chrome Web Store assets: 128x128 store icon, small promo tile 440x280, marquee 1400x560, and five screenshots at 1280x800 built from the redesigned UI (use docs/img as the source).
7. A one-page brand sheet: logo clear space, minimum sizes (16 px for the icon, 24 px high for the wordmark), colour values with tokens, do and don't examples.

CONSTRAINTS
- Keep the link with the current mark (the speech bubble and waveform idea, the indigo and the orange signal) unless you can show a clearly better concept. Original artwork only: no trademarked shapes, no marks from existing products. Run a quick similarity check against well-known meeting and transcription apps and say what you found.
- The product name is Debrief. Keep the wordmark source editable.
- Files must drop into the repo: extension/icons/ (icon16/32/48/128/512.png, the -glow variants, icon.svg), docs/brand/ (SVGs, brand sheet, store assets). Do not change manifest keys or extension ID.
- If the repo's scripts/make-icons.mjs is used to generate icons, update it to produce the new set so they can be regenerated.

ACCEPTANCE
- At 16 px in a real Chrome toolbar (light and dark theme) the mark is unmistakable and not muddy; show a screenshot.
- All exports are crisp (no blur from scaling) and share one silhouette across states.
- The logo works on white, on dark, and on a glass surface with a blurred colourful background.
