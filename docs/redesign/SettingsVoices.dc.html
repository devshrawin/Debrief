Voices

Put names next to what people said, by recognising their voice. Off until you turn it on. Everything here stays on this computer.

Recognise who is speaking

A best guess. When Debrief is not sure, the transcript says “Unknown speaker”.

Learn voices on its own

Debrief notices voices that come back in two or more meetings and asks you to name them. It can mix up people who sound alike, such as relatives or people on the same poor line. Tell your regular colleagues that you use this.

Keep short samples of unnamed voices

So you can press play and hear who it is before naming them. These clips are the only audio Debrief keeps: up to 8 seconds per unnamed voice, stored in this browser. A clip is deleted as soon as you name or dismiss that voice, and after 30 days in any case.

Privacy, in three facts

Stays in this browserRecognition runs on your computer. No voice is sent to Google, Claude or anyone else.
256 numbers per personA voiceprint is a list of numbers. It can’t be played back as speech.

Share voices with a colleague

Export saves the voiceprints (numbers, no audio) as a file a teammate can import, so their Debrief recognises the same people.

Only share a person’s voice if they have agreed. A voiceprint identifies someone, like a photo does.

Forget

Delete every voiceprint and sample on this computer. Names already in transcripts stay as written.
