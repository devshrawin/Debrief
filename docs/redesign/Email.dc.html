How Debrief fills it

Header block. Topic from the meeting title; Mode from the meeting app (Online, with the app name) or “Offline” when recorded in a room; Date as DD-Mon-YY, Time as 12-hour.

Attendees. Split into Client Side and System Integrator by email domain or the people list. Honorific (Sri. / Smt.), department and designation come from a saved directory; unknown ones stay as [DESIGNATION] to fill in.

Discussion table. One numbered row per topic, written in the formal register (“directed”, “apprised”, “noted”), with a Responsibility column.

Action items. Numbered, one bullet each; Debrief’s due dates go at the end of the sentence, because the template has no Due column.

Word and PDF. Borders and navy fills are set per cell, so Word keeps them; table rows never split across pages, and the header row repeats on every page.

Email. A short 600 px cover (topic, date, the action items table) with this document attached as PDF and Word.
