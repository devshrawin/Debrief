Shape, depth, motion

  • Cards 14 px, controls 10 px, chips and switches fully round.
  • Hairline borders do the work; shadow is barely there.
  • Motion only for live things: the REC dot, meters, the call glow. All of it stops under reduced motion.
  • Every target is at least 36 px; focus ring 2 px indigo, offset 2 px.
