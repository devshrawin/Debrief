Debrief: install (3 minutes)

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you will keep it (Chrome loads the extension from it; do not delete it afterwards).
2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder named "extension".
4. A set-up tour opens: paste a free Gemini key (aistudio.google.com/apikey, no card needed) and allow the microphone.
5. Join a Google Meet, click the Debrief icon, press Start recording this tab. Press Stop when done; the minutes are written for you.
   Started a recording by mistake? Use "Discard this recording" in the same panel: nothing is transcribed or kept.

Good to know
- The Debrief button opens Chrome's side panel, on every page. It stays open while you click the page or switch tabs.
- Settings > Appearance has the theme (match my computer / light / dark), the on-page recording pill and the toolbar recording icon.
- Needs Chrome 116 or newer. Tell people on the call that you are recording.
