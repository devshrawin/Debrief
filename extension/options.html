Setup guide

Get started

Debrief records a Google Meet tab, transcribes it, and writes the minutes. Set it up once in a few minutes. Ticks appear as each step is done.

  1. Add a Gemini API keyFree, no card needed. Create one at aistudio.google.com/apikey, then paste it below.
  2. Allow your microphoneOptional. Lets Debrief include your own voice. Open General
  3. Set your name and languageTags your lines and tunes transcription. Open General
  4. Email the minutes to yourselfOptional. Takes about two minutes. Open Email

Google Gemini

Used for transcription and for writing the minutes. Only needed if you choose Gemini on the next pages (the default).

Used automatically when the main key runs out. Google counts limits per project, not per key, so this only helps if the backup comes from a different project.

Debrief also learns on its own. After each meeting it remembers the names it saw (people in the call, the invite, the attendee list) as spelling hints, and it notices likely mishearings (a word that keeps appearing one letter off a real attendee's name) and offers them below.

          
One per line. Right spelling | misheard as, another fixes recurring mistakes (Amal | Aman). A plain line is a term to spell exactly. Used as a hint to the speech model, as a find-and-replace on transcripts, and by the minutes writer. If you list who was in a meeting on the minutes page, an alias that is really one of those people is left alone.

Voices

      
Teach Debrief what your regular colleagues sound like, so the transcript and minutes can say who spoke. This is a best guess, not a certainty.


      

        
Recognise who is speaking

        
        

          
When on, Debrief makes a small numeric fingerprint of each stretch of the other participants' speech, on this computer, and compares it with the voices below. Where it is unsure, or people talk over each other, the line says Unknown speaker rather than guessing. When Meet's own captions name a different person, both names are shown.

        

      


      

        
Learn voices on its own

        
        

          
While you record, Debrief fingerprints the other participants' speech and groups voices that keep coming back. A voice heard enough in two or more meetings shows up in the list below as Unnamed voice 1, 2…: you rename it once (check the transcript at the times shown to see who it is) and from then on it is named in every meeting, in any language. If Meet's captions are on and name exactly one speaker, Debrief also uses that to name the voice for you, but captions are optional. A voice is not used until it has about 45 seconds of clean speech from at least two meetings, and a group that sounds like two people or like someone already known is left out. Voices you record yourself are never changed. Similar voices can be merged or split wrongly, so check the names and delete anything that looks off. This builds a voice profile of the people on your calls: tell your colleagues, and you can delete anyone, or everyone, at any time.

        

      


      

        
Short samples of unnamed voices

        
        

          
This is the one case where Debrief keeps audio. While you record with learning on, short pieces of the other participants' speech wait in this browser (never uploaded); after the meeting one clip of about 8 seconds is kept for each unnamed voice and everything else is deleted. A clip is deleted when you name the voice, delete it, or press Forget all voices. People on your calls have not agreed to be recorded in this way by you alone: tell them, or leave this off.

        

      


      

        

          
          
        

      


      

        
Unnamed voices in your latest meeting

        

        
These voices matched nobody enrolled. The times are positions in the recording (also shown in the transcript on the minutes page): check who was speaking there, type the name, and save. Nothing is stored until you save.

      


      

        
Privacy

        

          
Everything happens inside your browser. The recording you make here is kept in memory only while this page is open, so you can play it back, and is never saved or uploaded. Apart from the optional short samples of unnamed voices below, only a fingerprint (a list of 256 numbers) is stored, in this browser's local extension storage, and it cannot be turned back into your voice. The speaker model (WeSpeaker ResNet34, 26 MB, CC-BY-4.0) is part of the extension, so nothing is downloaded and no audio is sent to any service for this. Forget all voices deletes every fingerprint, including the ones kept with past meetings.

Share voices

        

          

            
            
            
          

          

          
Lets a colleague use the voices you already enrolled instead of recording everyone again. The file holds fingerprints only (numbers, no audio), but a fingerprint still identifies a person's voice: share it only for people who have agreed, and only with someone you trust. Debrief checks that the same speaker model was used. Each person also has their own Export button in the list above.

        

      


      

        
Forget

        

          
          
Deletes every enrolled voice and every voice fingerprint saved with past meetings. Transcripts and minutes are not touched.
