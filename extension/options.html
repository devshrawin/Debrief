Google Gemini

Create one free at aistudio.google.com/apikey. No card needed. Used for transcription and for writing the minutes when you choose Gemini (the default).

Used automatically when the main key hits its daily limit.

Limits are counted per Google project, not per key. A backup key only helps if it comes from a different project.

Google's free tier may use your audio and text to improve its products, and reviewers may read them. For confidential meetings, use a paid key (billing on) or a local model in Speech to text and Minutes.

Keys stay in this browser profile. Audio and transcripts go directly from Chrome to the providers you choose; there is no Debrief server.

    Microphone

    Records your own voice as well as the other participants.

    When you mute yourself in Google Meet, Debrief stops transcribing your side as well.

    A small draggable control with the timer, a mic switch and alerts. Off by default so nothing covers the call.

    Browser permission

    Chrome asks once. Without it, only the other participants are recorded.

    Meeting pages

    Meet's live captions say who is speaking. Debrief uses that to put names to the minutes (your own transcript text is still used). Turning captions on shows subtitles on your screen. Who joined and left is read from the participant tiles either way.

    Zoom (in the browser), Microsoft Teams, Webex, Whereby, Jitsi, GoTo Meeting, BlueJeans, Amazon Chime, Zoho Meeting and Skype for Web. Debrief's toolbar icon glows amber during a call as a reminder to record, follows your mute button and stops when the call ends. Recording itself works on any tab. These apps use generic detection that may not fit every layout; "Check page detection" in the popup shows what it can read. The installed desktop apps cannot be recorded by a browser extension.

    Recording

    Detects Google Meet's "you left" and "meeting ended" screens, and leaving the call page. The minutes are then written (and emailed) without you doing anything.

    A safety net for when nobody (including you) has made a sound for that long, for example if Meet never shows an end screen.

    Names and terms

    One per line. Right spelling | misheard as, another fixes recurring mistakes (Amal | Aman). A plain line is a term to spell exactly. Used as a hint to the speech model, as a find-and-replace on transcripts, and by the minutes writer. If you list who was in a meeting on the minutes page, an alias that is really one of those people is left alone.

    Debrief also learns on its own. After each meeting it remembers the names it saw (people in the call, the invite, the attendee list) as spelling hints, and it notices likely mishearings (a word that keeps appearing one letter off a real attendee's name) and offers them below.

    Speech to text

    The service that turns the meeting audio into text.

    Provider

    gemini-3.5-transcribe handles Hindi and English switching. If your key has no quota for it, Debrief falls back to general Gemini models automatically. The key is set on the Get started page.

    From dashboard.sarvam.ai.

    Sent with language=multi for Hindi and English switching.

    Run it on this computer

    Any server that offers /v1/audio/transcriptions works (faster-whisper based servers, whisper.cpp server, LocalAI). Good for English and Hindi; for Malayalam use a Malayalam fine-tuned model. Setup guide.

    Groq: https://api.groq.com/openai/v1 · OpenAI: https://api.openai.com/v1 · local: http://localhost:8000/v1

    Minutes

    The model that reads the transcript and writes the minutes of the meeting.

    Provider

    From console.anthropic.com.

    Run it on this computer

    Nothing leaves your computer, and there are no quotas. Quality is lower than Gemini or Claude on messy Hindi, Hinglish or Malayalam, and a CPU-only PC takes a few minutes per meeting. Setup guide for local models.

    Ollama: http://localhost:11434/v1 · OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/api/v1 · Groq: https://api.groq.com/openai/v1

    Uses the Gemini key from the Get started page. If this model's daily quota runs out, Debrief tries other Gemini models.

    How carefully to write

    Thorough uses 3 or more model requests per meeting (more for long ones), so it costs more of a free quota. Short meetings always use one pass.

    Minutes format

    Layout of the minutes

    The official format fills the Amnex house template (header block, Client / System Integrator attendee tables, numbered discussion and action items, Next Meeting) for Print / PDF, Word and the email attachment. Debrief standard keeps the original minutes layout.

    Comma separated. People with these email domains are listed as the System Integrator; everyone else as Client Side. Leave blank to use the domain of your "Send to" address.

    One person per line: Name | Honorific | Department | Designation | Organisation. Add | Chair at the end for the usual chairperson. Unknown details stay as [DESIGNATION] / [DEPARTMENT] placeholders for you to fill in.

    Voices
    Put names next to what people said, by recognising their voice. Off until you turn it on. Everything here stays on this computer.
    

      
    
        
    Recognise who is speaking
    
        
    
          
    A best guess. When Debrief is not sure, or people talk over each other, the line says "Unknown speaker". When Meet's own captions name a different person, both names are shown. Debrief makes a small numeric fingerprint of each stretch of the other participants' speech, on this computer, and compares it with the voices below. Applies when you press Save changes.
    
          
    Debrief fingerprints the other participants' speech and groups voices that keep coming back. A voice heard enough in two or more meetings shows up below as Unnamed voice 1, 2…: you name it once and from then on it is named in every meeting, in any language. If Meet's captions are on and name exactly one speaker, Debrief also uses that to name the voice for you, but captions are optional. A voice is not used until it has about 45 seconds of clean speech from at least two meetings, and a group that sounds like two people or like someone already known is left out. Voices you record yourself are never changed. It can mix up people who sound alike, such as relatives or people on the same poor line, so check the names and delete anything that looks off. This builds a voice profile of the people on your calls: tell your colleagues, and you can delete anyone, or everyone, at any time. Applies when you press Save changes.
    
          
    So you can press play on any voice in the list below and hear who it is. These clips are the only audio Debrief keeps. While you record with learning on, short pieces of the other participants' speech wait in this browser (never uploaded); after the meeting one clip of about 8 seconds is kept for each learned voice (and for each person you record here) and everything else is deleted. A clip is deleted when you delete or merge the voice, or press Forget all voices. People on your calls have not agreed to be recorded in this way by you alone: tell them, or leave this off. Applies when you press Save changes.
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    
      
    

      
    
        
    Privacy, in three facts
    
        
    
          
    Stays in this browserRecognition runs on your computer. No voice is sent to Google, Claude or anyone else.
    
          
    256 numbers per personA voiceprint is a list of numbers. It can't be played back as speech.
    
          
    Gone in one clickDelete one person below, or everyone at the bottom of this page.
    
        
    
        
    The recording you make here is kept in memory only while this page is open, so you can play it back, and is never saved or uploaded. Apart from the optional short samples of unnamed voices, only a fingerprint is stored, in this browser's local extension storage, and it cannot be turned back into your voice. The speaker model (WeSpeaker ResNet34, 26 MB, CC-BY-4.0) is part of the extension, so nothing is downloaded and no audio is sent to any service for this. Forget all voices deletes every fingerprint, including the ones kept with past meetings.
